About Omicare

Dedicated to Your Health and Wellbeing.

At Omicare Multispeciality Hospital, we believe that quality healthcare begins with listening, understanding and caring for every individual. Our approach places patients at the heart of every interaction, with a commitment to respectful attention, professional medical consultation and a supportive healthcare environment.

We are dedicated to serving individuals and families in Okinni, Osogbo, and the wider Osun State community by providing accessible and reliable healthcare services.

To provide accessible, compassionate and patient-centred healthcare, supporting individuals and families through professional medical attention and a commitment to quality service.
